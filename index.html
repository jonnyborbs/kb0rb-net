About Me

Projects Equipment Contact

I come from a long line of hams going back generations and am proud to carry on the family tradition, combining technology and communication with the fun of meeting new people. I'm a member of the ARRL as well as the Vaca Valley Radio Club (VVRC) and Sac Valley Mesh, and got my FCC Amateur Extra in 2025.

The hobby is also where most of my software ends up. I build iOS apps for the things I wanted on my own bench — running FT8 from a phone in the field, and keeping an AllStarLink node under control from wherever I happen to be.

I'm always looking to chat with new folks and learn new things, so if you see me, say hi! I send eQSLs and haven't gotten physical ones made yet — but I hope to soon.

Software I write and support, mostly for the radio bench.

Greyline FT8

FT8 and FT4 digital modes for iPhone and iPad

A full-featured digital mode app that runs FT8 and FT4 straight from an iPhone or iPad. Features a live waterfall, automated QSO modes, POTA activations, spot maps and logging. All with no heavy laptop in the field.

Visit greylineft8.com

AllStar Node Control

Manage your AllStarLink node from your pocket

An iOS app for operating your AllStarLink nodes. Node Control lets you connect and disconnect links, watch node status, and keep your favourite nodes a tap away. Configure custom node macros and so much more.

Coming soon to the App Store

We Have Wine at Home!

A visual cellar for the wine, beer and spirits you already own

An iPhone and iPad app for cataloguing your home bar collection. Create and configure virtual wine cellars, add photos, track styles, quantities, locations, prices and more. CSV import and export keeps you in control of your data. Fully free. No accounts, no ads, no subscriptions.

Visit wehavewineathome.app
