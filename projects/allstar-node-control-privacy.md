This policy explains how the AllStar Node Control app for iPhone and iPad ("the app")
handles information. The app is developed and published by Jon Schulman, KB0RB ("I",
"me"), an independent developer.

## Information I collect

**None.** I operate no servers, no accounts and no back end for this app. I do not
collect, receive, store, sell, rent or share any personal information about you, your
device or your use of the app.

In Apple's App Privacy terms, the app's data collection is declared as
**Data Not Collected**.

## Information stored on your device

To do its job, the app stores the settings you enter. This information is kept on your
device only:

- **Node connection details** — the hostname or IP address, port number and any
  username, password or credentials you enter for each node you configure.
- **Saved and favourite nodes** — the list of nodes you have added, and any labels or
  ordering you give them.
- **App preferences** — display options and other settings you choose.

Credentials are held in the iOS Keychain, and other settings in the app's own private
storage. Neither is readable by other apps.

If you have iCloud Backup enabled for your device, this data may be included in your
personal encrypted iCloud backup. That backup belongs to your Apple Account; it is not
accessible to me.

**Deleting the app deletes this data** from your device.

## Network connections the app makes

The app connects only where you direct it:

- **Your nodes.** When you operate a node, the app connects directly from your device
  to the address you configured for that node, using the credentials you supplied.
  Nothing is relayed through any service of mine.
- **AllStarLink services.** If you use a feature that looks up node information, the
  app queries AllStarLink's public services directly from your device. Those queries
  are subject to AllStarLink's own privacy practices. AllStar Node Control is not
  affiliated with or endorsed by AllStarLink, Inc.

Whether a connection to your node is encrypted depends on how that node is configured;
the app cannot add encryption to a protocol that does not support it. If you reach your
node across the public internet, use a VPN or another secure tunnel rather than exposing
the node's management port.

## Analytics, tracking and advertising

The app contains **no** analytics, **no** crash-reporting services, **no** advertising
and **no** third-party tracking SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites,
and there is no in-app purchase or subscription data to collect.

## Diagnostics you may choose to share with Apple

If you have turned on **Share With App Developers** in
*Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements*, Apple may provide me with
aggregated crash logs and performance statistics for the app. This is a feature of iOS
that you control, and Apple's reports are anonymised and aggregated before I see them.
You can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Separately, if you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction
and gives me only aggregate sales and territory statistics. Apple's handling of that
information is covered by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and since I collect no information at all,
I do not knowingly collect information from children.

## Your rights

Privacy laws including the EU and UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give
you rights to access, correct, delete and port the personal information a company holds
about you, and to opt out of its sale.

Because I hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for me to produce,
correct, delete or sell. You are in full control of the data on your device: you can
view and change it inside the app, and remove all of it by deleting the app. I do not
sell or share personal information, and never have.

## Security

Credentials are stored using the iOS Keychain, which is encrypted by the operating
system and protected by your device passcode, Face ID or Touch ID. Because your data
never leaves your device, there is no account of yours for me to lose. Please protect
your device with a passcode, and use strong, unique credentials on your nodes.
