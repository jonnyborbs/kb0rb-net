AllStar Node Control puts your AllStarLink node in your pocket. Connect and disconnect
links, see what your node is doing, and keep the nodes you use most a single tap away —
without sitting down at a terminal.

## What it does

- **Connect and disconnect links** to the nodes and hubs you work with.
- **Live node status,** so you can see when your node is transmitting or receiving.
- **Saved nodes,** with the ones you use most kept a tap away.
- **Multiple nodes,** each with its own connection details.

## Your node, your data

The app talks directly to the nodes you point it at. There is no account to create, no
server of mine in the middle, and nothing about your operating gets reported anywhere.
Node addresses and credentials are stored on your device, and deleting the app removes
them.
