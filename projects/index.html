Projects

I build small, focused iOS apps — mostly for the radio bench, occasionally for the wine rack. No accounts, no ads, and your data stays on your device.

How I build things

Every app here is native, made for iPhone and iPad, and designed to work without a server behind it. Your logs, your cellar, and your node credentials live on your device — not in an account I hold. Where an app needs the internet, it talks to the service you asked it to talk to and nothing else.
