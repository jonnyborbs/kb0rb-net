AllStar Node Control

Manage your AllStarLink node from your pocket

Coming soon to the App Store

AllStar Node Control puts management of your your AllStarLink node in your pocket. Connect and disconnect links, see what your node is doing, and keep the nodes you use most a single tap away. Provides custom command macro recording, favorites lists, connected node views and more. Makes a perfect companion to your HT! All in a sleek, modern UI that makes operating AllStar fun.

What it does

  • Connect and disconnect links to the nodes and hubs you work with.
  • Live node status, so you can see when your node is transmitting or receiving.
  • Favorite nodes, with the ones you use most kept just one tap away.
  • Multiple nodes, each with its own connection details.
  • Custom macros, letting you take advanced control of your node

Your node, your data

The app talks directly to the nodes you point it at. There is no account to create, no cloud infrastructure in the middle, and nothing about your operating gets reported anywhere. Node addresses and credentials are stored on your device, and deleting the app removes them.
